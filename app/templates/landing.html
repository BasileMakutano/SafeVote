Your vote stays secret. Your ballot stays provable.

SafeVote runs student elections where nobody can vote twice, nobody can see how you voted, and everybody can check that their ballot was counted.

Vote See results

One person, one vote

Only students on the official roll can register, and the database itself rejects a second ballot from the same account.

Secret ballot

Who voted and what was voted live in separate tables. A ballot has no name, no timestamp and no link back to you.

Check your receipt

After voting you get a receipt code. Once results are published, you can confirm your ballot is in the final count.
